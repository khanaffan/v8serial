# Code of conduct

## Our standards

Contributors and maintainers must help keep project spaces respectful,
welcoming, and focused on constructive technical collaboration.

Examples of expected behavior include:

- giving actionable, respectful feedback;
- accepting differing experience levels and viewpoints;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them; and
- prioritizing the health of the community over individual preferences.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized conduct,
doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, and other
spaces where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report abusive GitHub content using GitHub's reporting tools. Project-specific
concerns may also be raised with
[@khanaffan](https://github.com/khanaffan). Do not publish sensitive personal
information in an issue.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Enforcement
decisions will consider context, severity, repetition, and impact.
